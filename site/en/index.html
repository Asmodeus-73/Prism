Frequently asked questions

Another question? Ask it on the project page.

Is Prism free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account.

Where are my photos saved?

On your device: on the computer for the app, in the browser online. No server. Clearing browser data clears the online library: the app is safer.

Does the AI send my photos anywhere?

No. The AI tools run on your device. Each model downloads once, on first use.

Does Prism work offline?

Yes, the Mac and Windows app. You need a connection to download an AI model the first time.

Which files can Prism open?

JPEG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, PSD, PDF, GIF, BMP, SVG, JPEG 2000 and RAW files from most cameras. Export to JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, GIF and as a Prism project (all layers).

Why does my Mac or Windows show a warning?

Prism isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft. On a Mac: right-click Prism, Open. On Windows: More info, Run anyway. Prism’s code is public.

Can I get back an older version of my edit?

Yes. Prism keeps a version every 15 minutes, and you can name your own. It all happens in the Versions panel.
