Frequently asked questions

Another question? Ask it on the project page.

Is Prism really free?

Yes: free, no ads and no subscription. You don’t need an account to edit.

Where are my photos saved?

On your device. In the app, on your computer; online, in the browser. Prism doesn’t send them to any server. Clearing your browser data also clears the online version’s library: to keep it, use the app.

Does the AI send my photos anywhere?

No. The AI eraser, subject detection and generative fill run on your device. Models are downloaded once, the first time you need them.

Does Prism work offline?

The Mac and Windows app works fully offline. You only need a connection the first time an AI tool downloads its model.

Which files can Prism open?

JPEG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, PSD, PDF, GIF, BMP, SVG, JPEG 2000 and RAW files from almost every camera. You can export to JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, GIF and as a Prism project (all layers).

Why does my Mac or Windows show a warning?

Prism isn’t signed by Apple and Microsoft yet. On a Mac, right-click Prism and choose Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. Prism’s code is public and open to review.

Can I get back an older version of my edit?

Yes. Prism keeps a version of each photo every 15 minutes, and you can name versions yourself. Go back, or open a version as a copy, from the Versions panel.
