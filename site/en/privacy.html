Privacy

Your photos stay on your device. No ads, no trackers, no analytics.

Your photos

The photos you open, your edits, your library and your settings stay on your device: on disk for the app, in the browser for the online version. No server receives them.

Artificial intelligence

The AI tools (AI eraser, subject and sky detection, generative fill…) run on your device. No photo is sent for analysis. Each AI model downloads once from GitHub, on first use.

What goes over the Internet

  • This site and the online version are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address with each page, like any host.
  • The typeface (Geist) comes from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
  • Downloads (the app, AI models, checking for the latest version) go through GitHub.

Nothing else: no usage statistics, no crash reports.

Prism account (coming soon)

An optional account will let you sync your photos between your devices. It isn’t open. Here is what it will store:

  • your email address, your hashed password and, if you add them, a name and a profile picture;
  • the list of your signed-in devices (device name and type);
  • while syncing, your photos stay on the server 24 hours at most, then are deleted. If both devices are on, they go from one to the other with no server;
  • the list of changes to sync (without the photos), kept for 30 days.

The server will be hosted by Cloudflare. You’ll be able to delete your account and this data. This page will be updated before the account opens.

Trial account

The trial account stays on your device: nothing is sent.

Your rights

To erase your data, delete photos from the library (then empty the Trash), or uninstall the app. Online, clear the site’s data in your browser. For a question, write on the project page.
