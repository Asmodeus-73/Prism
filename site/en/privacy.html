Privacy

In short: your photos stay on your device. Prism has no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

Your photos

The photos you open, your edits, your library and your settings are saved only on your device: on disk in the Mac and Windows app, in the browser’s storage for the online version. Prism doesn’t send them to any server.

Artificial intelligence

The AI tools (AI eraser, subject and sky detection, generative fill…) run on your device. Your photos aren’t sent to anyone for analysis. AI models are downloaded once from Prism’s GitHub page, the first time you use the tool.

What goes over the Internet

  • This site and the online version are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page.
  • The typeface (Geist) is loaded from Google Fonts, which therefore also receives your IP address.
  • Downloads (the app, AI models, checking for the latest version) go through GitHub.

Prism sends nothing else: no usage statistics, no automatic crash reports.

Prism account (coming soon)

An optional account will let you sync your photos between your devices. It isn’t open yet. When it is, this is what will be stored:

  • your email address, your password in hashed form (never in plain text) and, if you add them, a name and a profile picture;
  • the list of your signed-in devices (device name and type);
  • while syncing, your photos pass through the server for 24 hours at most, then are deleted. When both your devices are on at the same time, they go straight from one to the other;
  • the list of changes to sync (without the photos), kept for 30 days.

The server will be hosted by Cloudflare. You’ll be able to delete your account and this data. This page will be updated before the account opens.

Trial account

The trial account available today stays entirely on your device: nothing is sent.

Your rights

You can erase your data at any time: delete photos from the library (then empty the Trash), or uninstall the app. In the online version, clearing the site’s data in your browser removes everything. For any question, write on the project page.
