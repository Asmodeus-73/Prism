What’s new

What changes in each version of Prism.

Prism 1.0 New

Prism’s biggest update: a new interface and new tools.

A new interface

  • Card panels: tools, Navigator and Layers on the left, adjustments on the right.
  • Panels detach, move and snap left or right. The left panel folds away.
  • Workspaces: Develop, Retouch, Compose, or your own.
  • A new home screen: your latest photo shown large, recent photos, and drop photos anywhere on the window.
  • A clearer library: search, filters on one line, larger thumbnails, a tidy photo sheet.
  • On tablets and phones, a touch layout.

Your way

  • Settings (Ctrl ,): accent colour, Dark, Graphite and Night themes, corner rounding.
  • Customisable keyboard shortcuts.
  • Prism in English: View › Language.
  • A guided tour on first launch (Help › Guided tour).

Editing

  • Photo versions: a version every 15 minutes, named versions, go back or open as a copy.
  • 8 new effects: clouds, fractal, stained glass, frosted glass, organic warp, polar inversion, pencil sketch, oil painting.
  • Generative fill in the app: describe what’s missing and the AI draws it on your computer.
  • 16-bit everywhere: brush, eraser, clone, gradient, healing, smudge and paint bucket keep full precision.
  • The eyedropper picks the colour you see, adjustments included.

Faster

  • Import without re-encoding: a 24 MP photo is added in 2 seconds instead of 10.
  • The library opens without waiting, and double-click opens the right photo.
  • Quick slider preview on large photos.
  • Nearly empty layers take less memory.
